## Project Overview

A unified web-based tool called the Cleaner Schedule System was developed to simplify cleaning procedures in Universiti Malaysia Terengganu's (UMT) offices, lecture halls, and public spaces. While lecturers may easily check cleaning arrangements for their areas to plan a cleaning of their own room or table, cleaner supervisors can effectively assign cleaners, coordinate work, and monitor schedules.

In order to avoid overlapping assignments and missed cleaning duties, this system seeks to replace manual record-keeping and informal communication.
