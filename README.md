Software Engineering Group Project - Cleaner Schedule System

- **Course Code:** CSF3143
- **Course Title:** Software Engineering 
- **University:** Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT)
- **Academic Year:** 2026/2027 (1st Semester)

## Project Overview

A unified web-based tool called the Cleaner Schedule System was developed to simplify cleaning procedures in Universiti Malaysia Terengganu's (UMT) offices, lecture halls, and public spaces. While lecturers may easily check cleaning arrangements for their areas to plan a cleaning of their own room or table, cleaner supervisors can effectively assign cleaners, coordinate work, and monitor schedules.

In order to avoid overlapping assignments and missed cleaning duties, this system seeks to replace manual record-keeping and informal communication.

## Group Members and Responsibilities

| Member | Assigned Module | Roles and Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Member 1** | Cleaning Schedule Management | Identify schedule requirements and design the recurring weekly schedule functions and interface. |
| **Member 2** | Cleaner Task Assignment | Identify assignment requirements and design cleaner/task/location assignment functions. |
| **Member 3** | Cleaning Location Management | Identify location data requirements and design location maintenance functions. |
| **Member 4** | Schedule Monitoring, Reports & Feedback | Identify monitoring/reporting requirements; design request status, cleaning reports, and lecturer feedback functions. |
